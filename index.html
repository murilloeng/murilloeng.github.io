Biography

My name is Murillo Santana and I am an Assistant Professor at Aix-Marseille Université, where I work at the IUT GCCD department and the IUSTI laboratory. My research focuses on the stability and nonlinear behavior of structures, with an emphasis on tensegrity, deployable and morpho-plastic structures. By combining analytical solutions, geometrically and material nonlinear finite element formulations, and nonlinear dynamics, my work aims to predict the load capacity, instability, and dynamic response of lightweight mechanical systems.
